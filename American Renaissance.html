Andrew Breitbart Was Wrong

Kevin DeAnna, American Renaissance, October 14, 2026

With Scott Greer.

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:45
Instaread

Politics is not downstream from culture. Culture is downstream from power. Everything from regulatory policy to social movements to the current war against fraternities is ultimately about the determination of an elite to prevent alternative forces of power from arising. The culture war is eternal and its consequences are not just what kind of society we live in. It is the ultimate determinant of who gets what. Plus, Scott Greer joins to discuss his book Whitepill: The Online Right and the Making of Trump’s America and what has and has not changed on the American Right.

Topics: Alt-Right, Donald Trump, Race and Politics
